- **The lens inspects and edits fields, and it is itself a Wrela program.** Fields are functions over space that generate the world instead of baked assets. Clicking a rendered pixel traces it to the source that produced it in 0.9 ms. Dragging edits parameters live at 1.1–1.2 ms per solver step, 20–22 ms with a 512-pixel preview. The compiler knows nothing about the engine or the studio, by design.
- **Five rounds of agent-authored content were judged blind.** Pieces were rated on a four-tier scale: blockout, placeholder, shippable mid-tier, hero. The best result is an Opus-authored grazer at shippable mid-tier. The lens made no measurable difference to scores; the model used did.
- **Reference photos didn't help.** One round gave agents public-domain photos as reference material. Scores didn't improve. The recorded thesis now holds that the tools aren't yet the lever; the model and the medium are.
